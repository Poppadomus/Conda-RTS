# Asset and trademark policy

RTSMerge is designed to avoid distributing proprietary Command & Conquer assets.

Do not commit:
- extracted Westwood/EA sprite archives
- proprietary unit/building art
- Command & Conquer music or sound effects
- original game maps unless their license permits redistribution
- EA logos or branding

Users may provide their own legally obtained source assets locally. Any importer should transform only user-supplied files and should document the resulting format.

The gameplay code uses original names/descriptions where practical and should not imply endorsement by EA.
